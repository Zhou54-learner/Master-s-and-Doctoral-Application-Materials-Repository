Openings
We welcome outstanding candidates who are passionate about MXene, electrochemistry, and flexible electronics. Interested applicants are encouraged to send application materials to zhangdanzhen@westlake.edu.cn.
Postdoctoral Researchers, 2–3
We are recruiting postdoctoral researchers in MXene synthesis, electrochemical energy storage, spectroelectrochemistry, flexible electronics, and related interdisciplinary areas.
1. Requirements
Hold (or expect to obtain before the appointment begins) a Ph.D. degree in Materials Science and Engineering, Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Physics, Electrical Engineering, or a closely related discipline. (Applicants should be under the age of 35 in accordance with the institutional requirements)
Applicants with one or more of the following research backgrounds are particularly encouraged to apply: MXene synthesis and characterization; Electrochemical energy storage and in situ/operando electrochemical characterization; Micro-/nanofabrication or flexible electronic device fabrication; PCB design, embedded systems, wireless communication, signal processing, or related areas.
Applicants should have published at least one first-author paper in a peer-reviewed international journal in one or more of the above research areas
Demonstrated ability to conduct independent research, with strong critical thinking, scientific creativity, and problem-solving skills. Candidates should be self-motivated, collaborative, and committed to the highest standards of research integrity and academic ethics
) Excellent English communication, scientific writing, and presentation skills
2. Salary and Benefits
A competitive salary will be provided based on the applicant’s research experience and qualifications. The exact compensation will be discussed individually. The laboratory will provide a stable working environment, a first-class research platform, and support for applying to postdoctoral fellowship programs and related projects.
Westlake University can assist with household registration and help connect families with school and childcare services for children.
Westlake University provides a comprehensive postdoctoral training system and has established programs such as the Westlake University Outstanding Postdoctoral Fellow Award and the Westlake Outstanding Female Postdoctoral Fellow Award.
3. Funding Support
Postdoctoral researchers are eligible for Hangzhou municipal funding policies for in-station postdoctoral fellows.
For postdoctoral researchers who receive support from the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation or the Zhejiang Provincial Postdoctoral Excellence Program, Hangzhou provides 1:1 matching support.
Postdoctoral researchers who remain in, or come to, Hangzhou for employment after completing their postdoctoral training may receive a subsidy of RMB 400,000 per person from Hangzhou.
Eligible recent graduates with a bachelor’s degree or above who come to Hangzhou for work, including overseas returnees within five years of graduation and international talents, may receive living subsidies from Hangzhou. The subsidy for Ph.D. graduates is RMB 100,000.
Eligible applicants may apply for recognition as Hangzhou high-level talents and enjoy corresponding policies.
4. Application Instructions
Please send the following materials as PDF files to zhangdanzhen@westlake.edu.cn. The email subject should be: “Postdoctoral Application + Your Name”. Qualified applicants who pass the initial review will be contacted for an interview. This advertisement remains valid until the positions are filled.
Curriculum vitae

Administrative Assistant, 1
1. Job Responsibilities
Finance and Procurement
Handle daily reimbursement, laboratory consumables purchasing, and inventory management.
Follow financial regulations and participate in research budget preparation.
Laboratory Operation and Maintenance
Assist with laboratory setup, daily maintenance, and management.
Assist with training new group members. Applicants with a science or engineering background are preferred.
Maintain chemical reagent records and help write and update SOPs for laboratory instruments and equipment.
Research Documents and Meetings
Assist with the writing and revision of project documents.
Coordinate visits by invited experts and organize academic meetings; take minutes for selected important meetings.
General Administrative Affairs
Assist with recruitment, personnel-related coordination, and group-building activities.
Maintain the laboratory’s Chinese and English websites, organize academic activities, and manage research-related documents.
Additional Requirements
The candidate should be honest, responsible, professional, detail-oriented, and well organized. Strong interpersonal communication skills are expected. The candidate should have good language skills, basic English communication and writing ability, strong learning ability, a collaborative spirit, and a strong sense of responsibility.
2. Salary and Benefits
In accordance with Westlake University policies and the applicant’s qualifications, the laboratory will provide a competitive salary package, research-support conditions, social insurance and housing fund benefits, and other benefits provided by Westlake University. Specific terms will be discussed individually.
3. Application Instructions
Application period: This advertisement remains valid until the position is filled. Interested applicants are encouraged to submit their application materials as soon as possible.
Application materials: Please combine your CV and relevant supporting documents into a single PDF file and send it to zhangdanzhen@westlake.edu.cn. The email subject and attachment file name should follow this format: “Position Applied For - Your Name”.
Recruitment process: After preliminary review, shortlisted candidates will be contacted by phone or email for an interview. Applicants who do not receive an interview notification within three months may consider their application unsuccessful. Due to limited reception capacity, unsolicited visits are not encouraged.
