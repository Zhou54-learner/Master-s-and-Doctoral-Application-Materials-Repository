Principal Investigator
Danzhen Zhang
Assistant Professor, Principal Investigator
Department of Materials Science and Engineering, Westlake University
“Still waters run deep.”
zhangdanzhen@westlake.edu.cnGoogle ScholarCV
Danzhen Zhang received her B.S. degree in Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2019 and her Ph.D. degree from Drexel University in 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Yury Gogotsi, a pioneer in the MXene field and a leading expert in electrochemistry. She then conducted postdoctoral research at the Querrey Simpson Institute for Bioelectronics at Northwestern University, working with Prof. John A. Rogers. She has received honors including Beijing Outstanding Graduate, Tsinghua University Outstanding Graduate, and Drexel University Outstanding Ph.D. Graduate. She will join Westlake University full time in July 2027 as an Assistant Professor, Principal Investigator, and Ph.D. advisor.
