Emergent Electromagnetism at Nanoscale
Our Research
Emergent Electronic Orders
The interplay among lattice, orbital, spin, and charge degrees of freedom gives rise to a rich landscape of emergent electronic orders in Quantum Materials.
Our group employs a suite of experimental approaches, including crystal growth, optical spectroscopy, transport and scanning probes, to uncover the underlying mechanisms of the emergent electronic states.
We further investigate their responses to external stimuli, such as electric, magnetic and strain fields, with the goal of achieving controlled electronic properties.
Potential Information Applications
The study of emergent electronic phases offers new opportunities for next-generation information technologies, particularly in data storage and computation.
Our group designs novel device architectures, fabricates them at the nanoscale, and systematically evaluates their potential functionality.
The unique physics of the unconventional electronic phases could enable extraordinary properties, including ultrafast dynamics, high energy efficiency, robust endurance, substantial readout performance, compact integration, etc.
