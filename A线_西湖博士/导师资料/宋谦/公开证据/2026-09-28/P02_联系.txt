Work with us!
We are actively seeking passionate postdoctoral scholars and graduate students to join our dynamic team.
Postdocs
We seek passionate researchers to join our team in 2027. Experience in ultrafast optics, optical spectroscopy, crystal growth, or van der Waals device fabrication is preferred. Interested candidates should email Prof. Song with a CV and a brief summary of their research experience and interests.
Graduate Students
We are seeking self-motivated graduate students with background in Physics, Electrical Engineering, Materials Science and Engineering, or related fields. Prospective students should apply through the Westlake University Graduate Program and are encouraged to email Prof. Song with a CV and research interests.
Undergraduate Students
For current Westlake undergraduates, or undergraduate students from other universities interested in summer research, please email Prof. Song to discuss potential projects in the lab.
