Wenhao Wang, Ph.D. - Westlake University
Wenhao Wang was born in 1995 in the city of Hengyang, Hunan Province. He received his B.Eng. degree from the College of Science, Beijing University of Chemical Technology in 2016. He obtained his Ph.D. in Physics from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2022, after which he joined the Division of Physics and Applied Physics, School of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, as a postdoctoral research fellow. In February 2026, He joined the School of Engineering, Westlake University, as a tenure-track Assistant Professor (Principal Investigator and Ph.D. supervisor).

Terahertz waves (0.1–10 THz), located between microwaves and infrared radiation, possess unique characteristics such as high bandwidth, non-ionizing radiation, strong penetrability, and molecular fingerprint spectra. These properties endow terahertz waves with tremendous potential in 6G and beyond communications, biomedical imaging, and materials science. Dr. Wenhao Wang has long been devoted to fundamental research in terahertz photonics and to advancing its practical applications in wireless communications and sensing. He has published 10 first-author papers in high-impact journals, including Nature, Nature Photonics, Light: Science & Applications, Nature Communications, and Advanced Materials, with a total of more than 30 SCI-indexed publications. His research achievements have been featured by IEEE Spectrum, Science Alert, MIT Technology Review, and other international media outlets.
Looking ahead, Dr. Wang’s research group will continue to advance the field of terahertz photonics, including but not limited to:
1.     Developing next-generation on-chip and wireless terahertz communication systems;
2.     Designing reconfigurable multifunctional terahertz devices based on metasurfaces and photonic crystals;
3.     Exploring ultrafast terahertz photonics in emerging materials.
