Xinhao Li, Ph.D. - Westlake University
Xinhao Li received his bachelor's degree in 2015 from the Tsien Excellence in Engineering Program at Tsinghua University. He obtained his Ph.D. in 2021 from the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), under the supervision of Professor Nicholas X. Fang. From 2021 to 2024 and then from 2024 to 2025, he conducted postdoctoral research in the groups of Professor Dafei Jin at Argonne National Laboratory and Professor Amir Yacoby at Harvard University, respectively. In 2025, he joined the School of Engineering at Westlake University as a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronic and Information Engineering.

The group focuses on innovating novel qubit platforms grounded in fundamental material science, device physics, and quantum engineering principles. Specifically, through investigating the physics of cavity quantum electrodynamics, engineering the electromagnetic and phononic qubit interfaces, and advancing solid-state superconducting devices, we aim to achieve precise manipulation of electron qubits and facilitate scalable quantum information technologies. Current research directions include:
1). Investigation of interfacial and low-dimensional electron transport and exotic quantum phenomena;
2). Quantum control of electron charge and spin qubits on cryogenic noble gas substrates;
3). Integration and scaling of solid-state quantum devices, with applications in quantum computing, sensing, and communication.
