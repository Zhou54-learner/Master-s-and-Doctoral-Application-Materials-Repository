Shuigang Xu
PI
Dr. Shuigang Xu received his B.S. degree from Wuhan University in 2009, and Ph.D. degree from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in 2013. He worked as a postdoc in the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology from 2013 to 2016 and in University of Manchester from 2016 to 2020 supervised by Nobel Laureate Andre Geim. He joined School of Science, Westlake University in 2021.
