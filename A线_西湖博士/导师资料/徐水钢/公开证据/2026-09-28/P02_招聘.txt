Open positions
1. Assistant Researcher Fellow
Number of recruits: 3
Job Responsibilities: Carry out independent scientific research around the growth of two-dimensional materials, research on photoelectric properties, or the preparation and transport measurement of two-dimensional heterojunction devices, and participate in laboratory construction and management.
Application requirements:
Possess a doctoral degree in physics, materials or electronic science;
Love scientific research, work seriously and responsibly, be diligent and studious, and have good communication skills and teamwork spirit;
Good English reading, writing and communication skills.
2. Postdoctoral Fellow
Number of recruits: 2
Job Responsibilities: Carry out independent scientific research around the preparation and measurement of quantum devices based on two-dimensional materials and their heterojunctions, participate in laboratory construction and management, and help and guide doctoral students.
Application requirements:
Aged no more than 35 years old; have obtained or are about to obtain a doctoral degree;
Love scientific research, work seriously and responsibly, be diligent and studious, and have good communication skills and teamwork spirit;
Have certain English reading, writing and communication skills;
Priority will be given to those with the following experimental skills: A. Micro-nano processing technology; B. Low-temperature transport measurement and labview programming capabilities; C. Crystal growth or two-dimensional material transfer technology.
3. Research Assistant
Number of recruits: 2
Job Responsibilities: Responsible for the daily operation and maintenance of laboratory instruments; assist or independently carry out research work; learn and master core experimental techniques.
Application requirements:
Bachelor degree or above;
Be passionate about scientific research, have a sense of responsibility, and have strong self-discipline and hands-on ability;
Priority will be given to those with the following experimental skills: A. Material growth; B. Device preparation; C. Optoelectronics or transport measurement.
Have good communication skills and teamwork spirit.
Salary
According to the relevant regulations of Westlake University and the applicant’s work ability, the laboratory will provide competitive salary packages and scientific research conditions, and enjoy five social insurances and housing fund and related benefits of Westlake University. Specific treatment is negotiable.
