OPEN POSITIONS

We are seeking talented postdoctoral scholars, graduate students and research assistants to join the lab.


Postdoctoral Scholars

Two postdoctoral fellow positions are available starting Fall 2022. The qualified candidates must have or expect a Ph.D. degree in electronic, material science, or a closely related discipline. Applicants with strong expertise and experimental background in nano/micro-imprinting techniques, optoelectronic devices, and flexible biosensors are preferred.

The ideal applicant must be highly motivated and creative. Those interested should contact Dr. Wen (wenliaoyong@westlake.edu.cn), please send an email with a CV.


Graduate Students

Multiple Ph.D. graduate student positions are available starting Fall 2022. Applicants with background in Electronic, Materials Science and Engineering, or a closely related discipline are welcomed to apply. Please refer to Westlake University admission website for details.
