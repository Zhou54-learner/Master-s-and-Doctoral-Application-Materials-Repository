We currently have multiple openings for postdoctoral fellows, graduate students, and undergraduate students, please feel free to send email to liqiuyang@westlake.edu.cn. Looking forward to hearing from you!
Postdoctoral fellows: We welcome researchers who have (or will soon have) a PhD degree with research background of (including but not limited to) condensed matter physics, materials science, optics, physical chemistry, etc. to apply. The researchers with the below research backgrounds will be considered in priority: static/ultrafast spectroscopy, 2D van der Waals materials and devices (both photonic and electronic), low-dimensional nanomaterials (e.g., nanocrystals, 2D perovskites), LabView or Python for experimental setup control. We will offer highly competitive salary and support compared to equivalent positions both domestically and internationally (details to be discussed in person).
Graduate students：We welcome undergraduate students and master degree students who are interested in spectroscopy, low-dimensional or nano-materials, light-matter interaction, and cavity/photonic engineering to apply.
Undergraduate students: We welcome undergraduate students who are interested in spectroscopy, materials science, and optics to conduct research and do their internship in our group.
