May 15, 2026

In this work, we demonstrate the exponential convergence of our group's discrete variable local diabatic representation (LDR) method for nonadiabatic eigenvalue problems. Our results show that our LDR approach remains highly accurate and converges exponentially fast even for conical intersections where the traditional Born-Huang representation diverges, providing a robust, divergence-free framework for exact quantum dynamics simulations. Check it out!

In this work, we unveil the pivotal role of electronic quantum geometry—often neglected in previous studies—in governing both adiabatic and nonadiabatic topological quantum dynamics of molecular fiber bundles, and we show that the electronic overlap matrix alone encodes all essential geometric and topological effects including conical intersections. In this work, we develop the linked product approximation, a numerically efficient method to construct the global many-electron overlap matrix from products of nearest-neighbor overlaps, enabling accurate and nearly exact simulations of nonadiabatic conical intersection dynamics while drastically reducing computational cost. The time-convolutionless master equation is a powerful technique to model the non-Markovian dynamics of open quantum systems. In this arXiv, we introduce a diagrammatic representation of the time-local generator to arbitrary orders and also a non-perturbative method to approximate it by a nested time-ordered exponential.
