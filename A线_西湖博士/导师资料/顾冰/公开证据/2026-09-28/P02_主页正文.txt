In this work, we demonstrate the exponential convergence of our group's discrete variable local diabatic representation (LDR) method for nonadiabatic eigenvalue problems. Our results show that our LDR approach remains highly accurate and converges exponentially fast even for conical intersections where the traditional Born-Huang representation diverges, providing a robust, divergence-free framework for exact quantum dynamics simulations. The time-convolutionless master equation is a powerful technique to model the non-Markovian dynamics of open quantum systems. In this arXiv, we introduce a diagrammatic representation of the time-local generator to arbitrary orders and also a non-perturbative method to approximate it by a nested time-ordered exponential.
