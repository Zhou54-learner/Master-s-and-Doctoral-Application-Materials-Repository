Xinyi ZHANG 张心怡

zhangxinyi@westlake.edu.cn

♦ 2024 B.S. | Zhejiang Normal University

During my undergraduate years, I actively participated in various subject competitions related to the electronics major. I won the first prize in the Zhejiang Division of the National Undergraduate Electronic Design Contest and the third prize in the Zhejiang Division of the Intelligent Car Race. I also got exposed to deep learning algorithms and participated in provincial and university-level research projects such as the New Seedling Talent Project in Zhejiang Province.

In daily life, I am an enthusiast with a cheerful personality and diverse hobbies, including playing the piano, painting, baking, and traveling. As a new PhD student, I am well aware that the future academic path is full of challenges, but I am also full of anticipation and willing to keep exploring to become a better self!
