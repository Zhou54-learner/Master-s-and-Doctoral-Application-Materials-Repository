Qicheng "Scott" ZHANG
Email: zhangqicheng AT westlake.edu.cn
Biography page: English version, 中文版
Scott got his Bachelor's degree in Polymer Materials and Engineering at Zhejiang University and his Ph.D. in Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. He also got postdoctoral training in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at the University of Pennsylvania.
Scott is a fan of generalized puzzles. In his research, he enjoys finding connections between different fields and creating something new. Besides research, Scott likes to collect stories, folklore, and anecdotes and create new stories based on potential links between them. Unfortunately, he is, against his wish, a mediocre storyteller.
