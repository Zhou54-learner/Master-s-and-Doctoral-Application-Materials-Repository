Join Us

We are looking for more "investigators of the unknown" to join us!

We want a diverse and collaborative team to fulfill our tasks better. Our team members will first stand on their backgrounds, deepen their understanding, and then we will learn from each other and connect to more fields.

We welcome investigators from but are not limited to, electronic and information engineering, mechanics, physics, materials science and engineering, mechanical engineering, etc.

The openings below are long-term. We will contact eligible applicants for interview within two weeks. 


Graduate students

Please check here for details and application procedures. The Chinese version is here. 

The application usually has three rounds, starting in August and closing at the end of March.

Email Qicheng if you are interested in our lab and want to know more.


Postdoctoral Researchers

The postdoc candidates should have obtained or are about to get a Ph.D. and under 35 years old.

Preferred candidates will possess strong foundations in mathematics and physics, have experience in topological physics, or have experience in nanofabrication or microwave engineering.

To apply, please arrange for two reference letters and email the following  materials to Qicheng:

  • 1. Cover letter.

  • 2. Curriculum vitae.

  • 3. The contact information of your reference.

Or simply send a short email so we can start chatting!


Research Assistants

The research assistant condidates should have obtained or about to get a bachelor's degree or higher.

Please email Qicheng your

  • 1. Cover letter.

  • 2. Curriculum vitae.

Or simply send a short email so we can start chatting!
