Welcome to Solid State Ionics Lab!
We are an experimental materials science group at School of Engineering, Westlake University (Hangzhou, China) comprised of a few highly motivated group members working closely to study solid state ionic materials for energy and information applications. We combine advanced materials synthesis (e.g., Pulsed Laser Deposition for epitaxial oxide thin film growth) and characterizations (e.g. in situ and operando X-ray Photoemission Spectroscopy) to gain fundamental understanding and design new applications in the field of solid state ionics (SSI), applied surface science and solid state electrochemistry.
