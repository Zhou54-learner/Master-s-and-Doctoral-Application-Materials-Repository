Dr. Qiyang Lu (陆启阳)
Associate Professor (with tenure), PI
PhD Massachusetts Institute of Technology (2018)
BS Tsinghua University (2012)
Dr. Qiyang Lu was born and raised in Huai’an, Jiangsu, China. He received his bachelor’s degree from Tsinghua University (2012) and his PhD degree from Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT, 2018), both in Materials Science and Engineering. His PhD work was awarded best PhD thesis award from MIT. He did his postdoctoral study at Stanford University with a joint fellowship at Advanced Light Source, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. He received several prestigious awards, including Graduate Student Gold Award from Materials Research Society and Ross Coffin Purdy award from American Ceramic Society.
CV (Updated August 2026)

Dr. Ying Lu (陆颖)
Research Scientist (助理研究员)
PhD Shanghai Institute Of Ceramics, CAS (2018)
BE&ME South China University of Technology (2011, 2014)
Dr. Ying Lu was born and raised in Anhui, China. She received her bachelor and master degree from South China University of Technology, and her PhD degree from Shanghai Institute of Ceramics, Chinese Academy of Sciences on structural ceramics. After that She worked as a postdoctoral researcher at Shenzhen University on solid oxide cells. After working as a postdoctoral scholar in the SSI lab for two years, she is continuing her research on solid oxide cells and perovskite ceramics as a research scientist.
Email me: luying@westlake.edu.cn

Song Mao (毛松)
PhD Student (co-advised with Prof. Xiao Yang)
BS & MS Central South University (2019, 2022)
Song Mao was born and raised in Suzhou, Anhui Province. He received his bachelor’s degree and master’s degree from Central South University in 2019 and 2022, respectively, both in Metallurgical Engineering. He joined Solid State Ionic Lab in August 2022. As a PhD student, now he focuses on molten oxide electrolysis (MOE) and related oxygen evolution reaction (OER) on an inert anode. In the future, he wishes to reduce metal oxides with electrons, rather than with carbon or coke.
Email me: maosong@westlake.edu.cn
