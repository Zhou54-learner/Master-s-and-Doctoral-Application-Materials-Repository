PhD student positions
Full scholarships, including living stipent, housing allowance and research funds are provided to talented young researchers willing to undertake Westlake University PhD program. Our group is interested in doing research on the modelling of complex problems in earth and environmental sciences.
People with a background on physics, mathematics, civil and environmental engineering are welcome to apply.
Experience with modelling tools such as Lattice Boltzmann Method, Smooth Particle Hydrodynamics or similar Computational Fluid Dynamics methods
is highly desirable.
Research positions
We are interested in hiring talented scientists and engineers as posdoctoral researchers, with a phd degree in relevant fields, passionate about our research areas.
