## Subject

**Fall 2027 PhD inquiry — EM modelling for integrated photonic devices**

## Email

Dear Prof. Xu,

I recently completed a B.S. in Applied Physics at Harbin Institute of Technology and am seeking a Fall 2027 PhD opportunity. In my undergraduate thesis on resistive-film microwave absorbers, I initially focused on using a Python–CST workflow to search for better structural designs. While comparing thin-film and Ohmic Sheet models, I found that the modelling choice itself could change the predicted electromagnetic response and even the optimization outcome. That experience made me more interested in the modelling side of wave-based device design, rather than only in running an optimization.

Working from an existing workflow in our group, I added design variables and geometric constraints and automated simulation runs and reflection-data processing. This gave me practical experience with parameterized EM simulation and with adapting a computational workflow as the design problem changed.

I was interested in your group’s work on integrated photonic devices and photonic-electronic hybrid systems because I see a possibility to continue working on wave and device problems while moving into a new physical platform. As a bachelor-entry applicant, I hope to build the photonics background needed for that transition and to keep developing my experience with simulation-driven device research.

May I ask whether you think this background could be a reasonable starting point for a Fall 2027 PhD project in your group? I have attached my CV and undergraduate transcript and would be glad to discuss my previous work if helpful.

Thank you for your time.
